Privacy

This site collects nothing.

Something called PrivateAI should be private by example. Here is exactly what that means for this website.

What this site does not do

Nothing to accept, because nothing is taken.

No cookies
This site sets no cookies and uses no browser storage, not even to remember a preference.
No analytics or tracking
There are no analytics, counters, pixels or tracking scripts of any kind.
No requests to anyone else

The fonts, images and scripts all come from this site. Nothing is loaded from any other website.

Every page also tells your browser, through a Content-Security-Policy, to refuse anything from elsewhere. So this is enforced by your browser, not just promised.

No forms and no embeds
There is nothing to fill in, and no videos, maps or social media widgets that would report back to someone else.
No referrer
When you follow a link from this site to another one, your browser is asked not to tell the next site where you came from.
The moving rings
The moving rings on this site are drawn by your own browser and send nothing. You can pause them, and they stay still if your device is set to reduce motion.

What does reach someone

The host, and your email.

The web host

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub receives the technical details that every request carries, such as your IP address.

GitHub’s privacy statement explains what it collects.

If you email us

Email to support@privateai.ltd is delivered to our mailbox, hosted by Fastmail.
