About

Human-led, from Belfast.

PrivateAI is part of brooke.ltd, an independent creative and publishing ecosystem based in Belfast. Its tagline is Learn, Think, Discover.

The founder

Brooke Tamsin Clevenger.

Brooke Tamsin Clevenger is the Founder and Principal of brooke.ltd. She is a researcher, independent thinker, visual artist and philosopher.

She works across music, philosophy, visual art and systems design, as a solo operator.
